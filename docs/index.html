Ode to Aeterna, Rejoice for Memoria.

Preface序言

「寄蜉蝣於天地，渺滄海之一粟。」"We place our mayfly lives in heaven and earth — a single grain in the vast blue sea."

人類短暫的生命在永恆的宇宙面前，顯得何其渺小。時節更替，歲月流轉。我們在校園中追尋的青春，彷彿終將隨歲月消散。然而，縱使世間的永恆遙不可及，那些在生命中轉瞬即逝的美好，卻能如劃破夜空的流星，在記憶深處凝結成永不磨滅的光芒，於剎那間成就永恆。日後驀然回首，我們依然會因這份深藏於心底的溫暖而會心一笑。Against an eternal universe, a human life is brief and small. Seasons turn, years pass, and the youth we chase on this campus seems destined to dissolve with them. Yet even if eternity itself lies beyond our reach, the fleeting good in a life can streak across the night like a meteor and settle deep in memory as a light that never fades — eternity, made in an instant. Years from now, looking back, that warmth kept quietly in our hearts will still bring a smile.

Our vision我們的願景

Aeterna 正希望能成為大家記憶中的那顆流星。在短短一年的任期內，我們願為大家的校園生活抹上最絢麗的色彩，在漫長夜空中劃下一道最亮眼的光芒。我們期待透過多元化的活動與細緻的服務，將這段獨屬於大家的青蔥歲月凝聚成永恆，成為各位日後面對挑戰時源源不絕的動力。「落紅不是無情物，化作春泥更護花」，流星雖然終會落下，但它所化作的回憶，依然能滋養並照亮他人的心田。Aeterna hopes to be that meteor in your memory. In a term of only one year, we want to colour your school life as brightly as we can, and draw one clear line of light across a long night. Through varied activities and attentive service, we hope to gather these green years into something lasting — a source of strength when you meet challenges later on. As the verse goes, fallen petals are not unfeeling things: turned to spring soil, they nourish the flowers still to come. A meteor falls, but the memory it becomes can still light and feed the hearts of others.

「星星發亮，是為了讓每一個人有一天都能找到屬於自己的星星。」"The stars shine so that one day everyone may find their own star."

Aeterna 深信，我們帶來的微光能化為大家追尋自我的勇氣；在追隨星光的同時，各位亦能成為指引他人的璀璨星辰。因此，Aeterna 致力於為伊中學生搭建展現與發掘才華的舞台，透過一系列創新活動（如 Project: Imposter、QE 畫廊等），陪伴大家尋覓獨一無二的光芒。同時，我們亦會用心傳承伊中的傳統活動，延續這份美好。在此期間，我們將堅守崗位，定格大家的颯爽英姿，與各位攜手共建更美好的校園，留下最精彩的青春印記。We believe the small light we bring can become the courage to seek yourselves; and that in following the starlight, each of you may become a star that guides others. So Aeterna is committed to building stages where QE students can show and discover their talents — through new initiatives such as Project: Imposter and the QE Gallery — accompanying you as you search for a light that is entirely your own. At the same time we will carry our school's traditional events forward with care. Throughout, we will stay at our posts, capture you at your best, and build a better campus alongside you.

「天行健，君子以自強不息；地勢坤，君子以厚德載物。」"Heaven moves without ceasing; so the noble strive on. Earth bears all things; so the noble carry others with virtue."

Aeterna 將力求進步、盡善盡美，亦當包容萬物、追求共融。我們將秉承「修己善群」的校訓，傾聽每位同學的心聲，築起學生與學校之間最堅實的橋樑。憑藉敏銳的觀察與累積的經驗，我們定能凝聚力量、優化校園生活，為這段青春留下最真摯的感悟。Aeterna will strive to improve and to do things well, while remaining open and inclusive. Holding to the school motto — to cultivate oneself and serve the community — we will listen to every student and build the firmest bridge between the student body and the school. With careful observation and the experience we have gathered, we will bring people together, improve campus life, and leave these years with something sincerely felt.

中學生涯對我們每一個人而言，都承載著無可替代的重大意義。面對大家對中學生活的百般憧憬，Aeterna 在此鄭重承諾，我們必將全力以赴，讓這一年如同璀璨奪目的流星劃過歲月，為大家刻下最完美、最深刻的青春印記。Secondary school carries a weight none of us can replace. Faced with all that you hope this time will be, Aeterna promises this: we will give everything we have, so that this year passes like a brilliant meteor through the years — and leaves the deepest possible mark on your youth.

Ode To Aeterna, Rejoice For Memoria.

The full manifesto of the 28th Student Union, No. 3 Proposed Cabinet — what we will ask for, fix, run and answer for over the coming year.伊利沙伯中學第28屆學生會3號候選內閣詳細版政綱 —— 未來一年我們將爭取、改善、舉辦及交代的一切。

01

Vision願景

PREFACE序言

"Like a mayfly lodged between heaven and earth, a single grain in the vast ocean." Human life, so brief, seems utterly insignificant before the eternal universe. Seasons change, and years flow by. The youth we pursue on campus seems destined to fade with the passage of time. Yet even if eternity in this world is beyond reach, those fleeting moments of beauty can, like a meteor streaking across the night sky, condense into an inextinguishable light deep within memory — achieving eternity in an instant. When we suddenly look back one day, we will still smile knowingly because of this warmth hidden deep in our hearts.「寄蜉蝣於天地，渺滄海之一粟。」人類短暫的生命在永恆的宇宙面前，顯得何其渺小。時節更替，歲月流轉。我們在校園中追尋的青春，彷彿終將隨歲月消散。然而，縱使世間的永恆遙不可及，那些在生命中轉瞬即逝的美好，卻能如劃破夜空的流星，在記憶深處凝結成永不磨滅的光芒，於剎那間成就永恆。日後驀然回首，我們依然會因這份深藏於心底的溫暖而會心一笑。

VISION願景

Aeterna hopes to become that meteor in everyone's memory. Within a term of just one year, we wish to paint the most brilliant colours onto everyone's campus life, and carve the most dazzling streak of light across the long night sky. Through diverse activities and meticulous service, we hope to condense this youthful chapter — unique to each of you — into eternity, becoming an endless source of strength when you face challenges in the days ahead. "Fallen blossoms are not heartless things; turned to spring soil, they nurture the flowers anew." Though a meteor must eventually fall, the memories it becomes can still nourish and illuminate the hearts of others.Aeterna 正希望能成為大家記憶中的那顆流星。在短短一年的任期內，我們願為大家的校園生活抹上最絢麗的色彩，在漫長夜空中劃下一道最亮眼的光芒。我們期待透過多元化的活動與細緻的服務，將這段獨屬於大家的青蔥歲月凝聚成永恆，成為各位日後面對挑戰時源源不絕的動力。「落紅不是無情物，化作春泥更護花」，流星雖然終會落下，但它所化作的回憶，依然能滋養並照亮他人的心田。

OUR PROMISE我們的承諾

"As heaven moves vigorously, the gentleman strives endlessly for self-improvement; as the earth's terrain is vast and yielding, the gentleman carries all things with great virtue." Aeterna will strive for progress and excellence while embracing all things and pursuing inclusiveness. We will uphold the school motto of cultivating oneself and benefiting the community, listen to the voice of every student, and become the warmest and most solid bond between students and the school.

Our secondary school years carry an irreplaceable significance for each of us. Facing everyone's countless hopes for their secondary school life, Aeterna solemnly promises to give our all — to make this year streak across the years like a dazzling meteor, engraving the most unforgettable mark of youth upon everyone's memory.「天行健，君子以自強不息；地勢坤，君子以厚德載物。」Aeterna 將力求進步、盡善盡美，亦當包容萬物、追求共融。我們將秉承「修己善群」的校訓，傾聽每位同學的心聲，成為學生與學校之間最溫暖堅實的紐帶。憑藉敏銳的觀察與累積的經驗，我們定能凝聚力量、優化校園生活，為這段青春留下最真摯的感悟。

中學生涯對我們每一個人而言，都承載著無可替代的重大意義。面對大家對中學生活的百般憧憬，Aeterna 在此鄭重承諾，我們必將全力以赴，讓這一年如同璀璨奪目的流星劃過歲月，為大家刻下最完美、最深刻的青春印記。

02

Advocacy爭取

Six things we will ask the school for, and keep asking until there is an answer.六項我們將向校方爭取的事項，直至有明確答覆為止。

01

Extension of library opening hours for the two weeks before exams延長考試前兩週的圖書館開放時間

Library hours follow no clear, standard schedule, so students are often left without enough time to study. We are advocating for hours to be extended uniformly to 18:00 in the two weeks before exams — ample time after class to read or study quietly, a better learning atmosphere on campus, and habits of independent learning.現時圖書館開放時間未有明確統一標準，同學往往未有足夠時間溫習。我們爭取將考試前兩周的開放時間統一延長至18:00，讓同學課後有充裕時間閱讀或安靜溫習，藉此提升校園學習氛圍，並鼓勵自主學習習慣。

02

School-wide Canva Education access向校方申請 Canva 教育版

Canva is a design tool many students already use for presentations and posters, but much of it sits behind payment, which limits what students can make. We will advocate for the school to apply for Canva Pro for Education, giving every student free access to the premium features, content and graphics.Canva 是近年流行的線上平面設計工具，不少同學平日也會用以製作簡報、設計壁報等。惟裡面包含大量付費解鎖內容，限制學生的創作。我們將爭取讓校方申請 Canva Pro 教育版，令全校學生都可免費享用付費版功能、內容和圖像，擴闊同學的創作空間。

03

Allowing uniformed group members to wear their uniforms to school允許制服團隊穿著制服回校

Members of uniformed groups are currently required to arrive in school uniform on event days, yet their group uniforms are intricate and changing on campus takes a long time. We will advocate for members to wear their group uniforms directly to school on those days.不少制服團隊成員於活動日仍需穿著校服回校。然而，團體制服設計繁複，於校內更換相當耗時。為此，我們將爭取讓制服團隊成員於活動日直接穿著制服回校，以提升活動效率。

04

A community digital piano in the covered playground於有蓋操場設置共享電子鋼琴

To ease academic pressure and open a route to relaxation, we are working to place a shared digital piano in the covered court — somewhere anyone can play freely, connect through music, and warm the campus with a little more art in it.為舒緩同學的課業壓力並提供放鬆的渠道，我們將爭取於有蓋操場設置共享鋼琴，期望大家能隨心演奏，透過音樂互相交流，拉近彼此距離，為校園增添溫馨的藝術氣息。

05

A canopy where the rear gate meets the shopping mall學校後門與商場交界處加設簷篷

The narrow passageway at the rear entrance gets congested whenever it rains. We will push for a canopy there, giving staff and students room to open and close umbrellas and keeping the passageway clear.由於學校後門通道狹窄，導致雨天時常出現擠塞。我們將爭取於學校後門增設簷篷，增加師生開收雨傘的空間，以保持通道暢通，方便師生進出。

06

Access to the previous year's internal exam papers老師給予同學上年度校內試卷

We will approach the school and the panel heads and teachers of every subject — Chinese, English, Mathematics, Liberal Studies / Citizenship and Social Development, and the electives — for permission to publish last year's question papers without answer keys. Students wanting the answers visit the library. The Student Union will keep a dedicated Google Drive, organised clearly by form and subject, so papers are there whenever you need them.我們將主動向學校及各學科（包括中、英、數、通識／公社科及各選修科）的科主任和老師進行溝通，爭取授權發布最近一年的考試問題卷，並不設有答案。若同學需要答案以作參考，需自行前往圖書館。學生會將會建立一個專屬的雲端 Google Drive，按照「年級」和「科目」將問題卷清晰上載，方便同學隨時溫習。

Improvements改善

The small, unglamorous fixes that decide what a school day actually feels like.不起眼卻實在的修補，決定了每一個上學日的感受。

01

Classroom and laboratory environments改善課室及實驗室環境

Classroom cabinets班櫃

External personnel come and go through our premises often. To keep each class's materials safe, we will fit every classroom cabinet with a lock.鑒於本校舍外來人員進出頻繁，為確保各班物資安全，我們將為每班班櫃配置鎖頭，以杜絕遺失風險。

Desks and chairs桌椅

Some desks and chairs wobble, which can pull a student's attention out of the lesson. We will collect opinions, speed up and standardise the follow-up process, and see that equipment is ready for the new school year.部分桌椅現時出現搖晃不穩的問題，恐影響學生上課專注。我們將收集同學意見，加快及統一跟進流程，確保新學年設備完備。

Fans風扇

Some fans have aged badly — they rattle in use, or fail to start at all. We will collect opinions together and speed up follow-up so they stop being a distraction.部分風扇老化問題嚴重，運作時或會發出異響，甚至無法開啟，影響學生上課專注。我們將會統一收集意見，加快跟進流程，避免造成困擾。

02

SU CounterSU Counter

Duty當值問題

Students often need photocopying and are too often met with a broken machine or an unattended counter. The self-discipline of the students on duty is what keeps the counter running, so we are redesigning a stricter duty system to make sure someone is always there.留意到同學們常有影印需求，卻不時因影印機故障或 SU Counter 無人駐守而大感困擾。我們深切明白，值班同學的自律是維持 SU Counter 運作的根本。因此，我們正重新規劃更嚴格的值班機制，確保長期有人值勤。

Automated printing影印服務自動化

We will place a self-service photocopier at the SU Counter: students pay for it themselves and follow the instructions beside it. QES students hold to the school motto of cultivating oneself and serving the community, so we have chosen a trust-based system — the fastest, most convenient way to print.
Lunch on school days (12:55–13:45) and after school.我們將在 SU Counter 放置自動影印機，由學生自行付費，根據一旁的指引影印所需物資。我們相信 QES 學生秉持「修己善群」的傳統校訓，故選擇採取信任制，以最大化打印的效率，便利同學。
時間：上學日的午飯時段（12:55–13:45）及放學後。

Replacing the printer更換影印機

Breakdowns are the root of the trouble, not the queue. We will lobby the school to replace the printer outright, so printing needs are met quickly and for good.留意到同學們常有影印需求，卻因影印機故障而大感困擾。我們將積極向學校爭取更換影印機，期望透過引入全新設備，更快捷、根本地解決大家的打印需求。

03

School uniform material改善校服材質

Female students' winter uniform shirts are thin and see-through. We will press the uniform supplier to improve the material, and push for only the improved shirts to be sold.女同學冬季校服恤衫材質較薄，透光性高。有見及此，我們將和校服供應商加強溝通，要求改善女同學冬季校服恤衫的材質，爭取全面售賣材質調整後的恤衫，減少同學困擾。

04

Washrooms改善洗手間問題

Washrooms run out of water from time to time, which ruins the experience. We will report facility problems to the General Office so the school can follow up as soon as possible.洗手間不時出現缺水的情況，嚴重影響如廁體驗。我們將向校務處申報設施問題，讓校方儘快跟進。

Communications溝通

Proceeds return in full within a week of a successful sale, and unsold items come back to you.家中有些物品已經用不上，但丟掉又似乎很浪費……你眼中的廢物在別人眼中或許仍有價值！Aeterna 將在本年度學年結束的前夕舉辦「伊中跳蚤市場」，提供機會讓同學售賣用不上的物品。只要物品在經過幹事檢查後，確認符合類別和新舊程度要求，我們便會收集物品並代為售賣。物品的金額將在交易成功後一星期內全數歸還給物品原主，未能成功賣出的物品亦將退還賣家。

Countless students here draw well, yet few get to show it on campus. Joint-School Oral Practice聯校口語練習
