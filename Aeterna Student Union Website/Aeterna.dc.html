01 Transparency in everything we spend

Placeholder. A termly public ledger of union income and expenditure, posted on this site within two weeks of each cycle closing, with receipts for anything above a set threshold and a short written note on every decision we took to spend it.

02 Academic support that reaches first-years

Placeholder. A peer-mentoring pairing run in the first month, a shared archive of past papers and notes by department, and a standing channel to raise grading and scheduling issues without going through six offices first.

03 Hostel and mess conditions, taken seriously

Placeholder. Monthly mess feedback that actually changes the menu, a maintenance tracker students can see, and a published escalation path when a complaint sits unanswered for more than a week.

04 Clubs funded fast, not favourably

Placeholder. One application form, a published rubric, a two-week decision deadline, and a shared inventory so three clubs stop buying the same speaker every year.

05 Safety and wellbeing on campus

Placeholder. Lighting and transport audits walked at night by students, a counselling route that doesn't require a professor's signature, and a confidential reporting channel with a named person responsible for it.

06 Sports and fitness beyond the team sheet

Placeholder. Open ground slots published weekly, equipment that can be borrowed without a captain's approval, and intramural leagues for people who are not on any team.

07 A greener, quieter campus

Placeholder. Refill stations before plastic bans, segregated waste that is actually collected separately, and a yearly audit published alongside the budget.

08 Accountability after the election

Placeholder. This page stays up. Every promise above gets a status — done, in progress, blocked, with the reason — updated each term so you can hold us to the exact words we used.
