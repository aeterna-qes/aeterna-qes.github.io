Aeterna

Eighteen students, one cabinet. Aeterna means lasting — we want to leave behind things that outlive our term, not slogans that end with it. 十八位同學，一個內閣。Aeterna 意為「恆久」——我們希望留下能延續下去的東西，而不是隨任期結束的口號。

Who we are我們是誰

A short introduction簡短的自我介紹

Aeterna came together in a common room, out of a shared frustration: good ideas on this campus keep dying in inboxes. We are students from different years and departments — organisers, designers, athletes, writers — who decided it was easier to fix that from inside the union than to complain about it from outside.Aeterna 始於一次共用空間裡的閒談，源於一個共同的挫折：校園裡許多好想法，最後都止於無人回覆的信箱。我們來自不同年級與學系——統籌、設計、運動、寫作——最終決定，與其在外面抱怨，不如走進學生會把問題解決。

Our approach is unglamorous on purpose: publish what we spend, answer what we're asked, finish what we start. Everything we intend to do is written below in full, so you can hold us to the exact words.我們的做法刻意樸素：公開每一筆支出、回覆每一個提問、完成每一件承諾。以下是我們全部的政綱，請以這些文字檢驗我們。

What we're running on我們的主張
01

A union you can reach找得到人的學生會

Published office hours, one inbox, and a public log of every request and what happened to it.公開的當值時間、單一聯絡信箱，以及每宗訴求及其處理結果的公開紀錄。

02

Money in the open帳目公開

Every unit of union spending posted each term, line by line, before anyone has to ask.每學期逐項公開學生會所有支出，不必等人追問。

03

A campus that works late深夜也運作的校園

Longer study hours, lit walkways, working facilities, and a canteen menu students vote on.延長自修時間、照明充足的通道、可用的設施，以及由同學投票決定的餐單。

04

Clubs first, ceremony later先支援學會，再談排場

Faster fund approvals, shared equipment, and events planned with clubs instead of around them.加快撥款審批、共用器材，並與學會共同策劃活動，而非繞過他們。

01 Transparency in everything we spend財政全面公開

Placeholder. A termly public ledger of income and expenditure, posted within two weeks of each cycle closing, with receipts above a set threshold and a short note on every decision to spend.示例內容。每學期公開收支帳目，於結算後兩週內上載，超過指定金額附上單據，並就每項開支決定附簡短說明。

02 Academic support that reaches everyone惠及每位同學的學術支援

Placeholder. Peer mentoring paired in the first month, a shared archive of past papers and notes, and a standing channel for grading and timetable issues.示例內容。開學首月配對朋輩導師、設立歷屆試題與筆記共享庫，並設常設渠道反映評分及時間表問題。

03 Campus facilities, taken seriously認真對待校園設施

Placeholder. Monthly canteen feedback that actually changes the menu, a maintenance tracker students can see, and a published escalation path for anything unanswered after a week.示例內容。每月收集餐廳意見並真正調整餐單、設立同學可查閱的維修進度表，以及公開的跟進機制——逾一週未獲回覆即自動升級處理。

04 Clubs funded fast, not favourably撥款要快，而非講人情

Placeholder. One application form, a published rubric, a two-week decision deadline, and a shared inventory so three clubs stop buying the same equipment every year.示例內容。統一申請表格、公開評審準則、兩週內完成審批，並設共用器材清單，避免多個學會每年重複購置同樣設備。

05 Wellbeing and a safer campus身心健康與校園安全

Placeholder. Lighting and route audits walked by students, a counselling path that doesn't need a teacher's signature, and a confidential reporting channel with a named person responsible.示例內容。由同學實地巡查照明及通道、無須老師簽署即可使用的輔導途徑，以及設有指定負責人的保密舉報渠道。

06 Sports and fitness beyond the team sheet不只校隊的運動機會

Placeholder. Open ground slots published weekly, equipment borrowable without a captain's approval, and casual leagues for people not on any team.示例內容。每週公佈開放場地時段、借用器材毋須隊長批准，並為非校隊同學舉辦友誼聯賽。

07 A greener campus更環保的校園

Placeholder. Refill stations before plastic bans, waste that is actually collected separately, and a yearly audit published alongside the budget.示例內容。先增設斟水機再談禁膠、確保回收物真正分開收集，並每年連同財政報告公佈環保檢討。

08 Accountability after the election當選之後的問責

Placeholder. This page stays up. Every promise above gets a status — done, in progress, blocked, with the reason — updated each term so you can hold us to the exact words.示例內容。本頁將持續保留。以上每項承諾都會標示進度——已完成、進行中、受阻（並附原因）——每學期更新，讓同學以原文檢驗我們。
